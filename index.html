② Corporate cards · Credit line

Cards are just the start.
Add credit at no interest.

Ark gives you debit cards. Jeeves gives you cards backed by a no-interest credit line: you keep a fixed deposit and spend beyond it, and the balance above your deposit is settled at month end. The cards sit in the same platform as your supplier payments, so you see everything in one place.

🏦

No-interest credit line

Keep a fixed minimum in your account (about 30%) and spend above it. For example, leave US$20K fixed and spend up to US$50K in the month. At month end Jeeves collects what you spent above your deposit, and the line renews.

💳

Physical & virtual cards

There's no issuance or annual renewal fee. Cards use US BINs, with options based in Mexico or Brazil, and can be assigned to each user, then frozen or re-limited in one click. Physical cards ship anywhere, including to a teammate in Peru.

🤖

AI expense management

Each cardholder snaps a photo of the receipt. The AI captures it, matches it to the transaction and books it automatically, so expenses are tracked without spreadsheets.

🌍

Spend in any currency

Jeeves adds no fee for card purchases in other currencies; you pay only Visa's exchange rate. Cashback starts at 2%, and we're working on an improved rate as part of a broader agreement.
